# Lexicon Delver - Decisions

Status: CURRENT

## D-001 - Lexicon Delver is the only active word-merge concept
Status: ACTIVE
Lexicon Delver remains the active concept. Compound Forge and Chain Reaction are historical only unless explicitly reopened.

## D-002 - Individual words are tactical resources
Status: ACTIVE
A source word may participate in multiple possible combinations. Choosing one consumes that word and removes its other uses for the current encounter. Opportunity cost is central.

## D-003 - Semantic merging is not limited to strict dictionary compounds
Status: ACTIVE
Compositional phrases such as LIGHT SHIELD and BREAK ARMOR are valid when the combined phrase communicates a coherent effect.

## D-004 - Word meanings communicate mechanical roles
Status: ACTIVE
FIRE communicates damage/burning/retaliation; ICE blocking/delay/protection; LIGHT reveal/detection; BREAK physical-defense removal; forms retain their validated roles.

## D-005 - Semantic function outranks exact numbers during validation
Status: ACTIVE
Small fixed numbers exist to make scenarios executable. Exact tuning may change later, but semantic roles must remain stable within an experiment.

## D-006 - Immediate validation is about semantic tactical reasoning
Status: ACTIVE
The primary validation question is whether the word system creates choices the player can reason about. General fun, progression appeal, marketability, and production polish are separate questions.
