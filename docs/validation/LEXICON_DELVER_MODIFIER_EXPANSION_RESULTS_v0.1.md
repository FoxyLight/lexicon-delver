# Lexicon Delver Modifier Expansion Experiment v0.1 — Results and Decision

Status: Authoritative
Date: 2026-09-24

## Decision

PIVOT

## ME-1

Blind semantic transfer was strong. The player predicted coherent contact-punishment behavior for THORN WALL, THORN SHIELD, THORN ARMOR, and THORN TRAP. THORN BALL was also expected initially, but its exclusion felt reasonable after the reactive-contact boundary was explained.

## ME-2

Chosen move: THORN WALL, right lane.

The player chose THORN over FIRE because THORN dealt more damage, and chose WALL over SHIELD because WALL dealt more damage. Although FIRE and THORN still felt meaningfully different at the language level, the tactical choice reduced primarily to numerical comparison.

## ME-3

Chosen move: THORN SHIELD.

Alternative: FIRE SHIELD.

The player again preferred THORN because it dealt more retaliation damage, while SHIELD was chosen for damage mitigation. The player expected the Caster specifically to take retaliation damage; learning that THORN SHIELD retaliates against whichever enemy first triggers it was mildly surprising.

After THORN and SHIELD were consumed, the remaining visible pool was FIRE, LIGHT, WALL, TRAP. The planned follow-up was LIGHT WALL to reveal hidden enemies. Opportunity cost remained legible.

## Rationale

The experiment passes semantic transfer but fails the intended FIRE-versus-THORN differentiation strongly enough to block CONTINUE.

Across both tactical scenarios, THORN repeatedly functioned as FIRE with different damage numbers. This is direct evidence for:

- F-06: numerical substitution
- F-07: tactical redundancy

The problem is narrow rather than systemic. Recipe dependence did not appear, form identity remained understandable, BALL exclusion did not create persistent friction, and mixed-hand consumption remained legible.

Therefore the correct decision is PIVOT, not KILL.

## Pivot target

Preserve the validated baseline and the broader modifier-expansion approach.

Do not add another modifier yet.

Change only the new modifier design so that its tactical identity is not primarily direct damage competition with FIRE on the same triggers.

The corrected modifier should:

- remain semantically predictable,
- reuse existing combat primitives,
- work across multiple established forms,
- avoid new subsystem complexity,
- create non-numerical tactical differentiation from FIRE.

The THORN SHIELD targeting surprise is a secondary watch item and does not justify a global rule change.

## Next authorized question

Design a minimal v0.1.1 modifier correction that changes only the failing THORN mappings or replaces THORN if necessary with a semantically cleaner modifier. Do not expand vocabulary further until the corrected modifier passes.
